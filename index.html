How to use

A to-scale model of the theater, built from the house plans

  1. Look around. Drag to spin the room. Pinch (or scroll) to zoom in and out. The square button in the corner fits the whole room back on screen.
  2. Three views, top right. 3D is the whole room from above. Walk puts you inside: push the round pad to walk, drag to look around, and tap Sit down to see what a seat sees. Plan is the floor plan.
  3. Move furniture. Tap a piece to pick it, then drag it. Drag the round arrow to turn it. Options has its size, color and shape, and Delete.
  4. Add furniture. Tap Add, choose something, then tap the floor where it goes (or a wall, for TVs and shelves).
  5. Room and lights. Room changes the floor, wall and ceiling colors. The light bulb switches between Bright, Dim and Movie.
  6. Mistakes. Undo takes back the last change.
  7. Save and share. In Layouts, tap Save to keep an arrangement on this phone or computer. Send link to this layout lets you text it to someone, and they see exactly what you made.

Everything is drawn to scale from sheet 8 of the plans, so furniture sizes and distances are real. Tap the ? next to the title to see this again.
