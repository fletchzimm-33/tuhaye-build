Welcome to Ridgeline Residence

A to-scale 3D model of the home, built from its architectural plans.

  • Pick what to seeExterior shows the whole home on its site. Main level, Lower level and Theater open up each floor.
  • Three ways to look3D spins the model. Walk puts you inside at eye height: use the round pad (or the arrow keys) and drag to look around, through doorways, up the stairs and out onto the decks. Plan is the floor plan with every room named.
  • Try a different layoutFurniture stays put while you look around. Tap Edit furniture, then drag a piece to move it or turn it with the round arrow. Add brings in new pieces at real sizes, and Undo takes back a change.
  • Finishes and lightFinishes changes the theater's floor, walls and ceiling. The light button moves the day from midday to dusk, or dims the theater for a movie.
  • Save and shareLayouts keeps an arrangement and makes a link you can send, so others see exactly what you made.
